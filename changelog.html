The app takes center stage

August 28, 2026

Added

  • First concept for the Pravely app home screen.
  • Product direction centered on cash flow, next steps, debt progress, and goals.
  • A free-account path that will connect the Essentials workbook to future app access.

Changed

  • Pravely is now presented as an app rather than a collection of paid workbooks.
  • The Essentials workbook becomes the free starting point for account holders.
  • Website colors, typography, and product previews now reflect the app direction.

Exploring

  • Debt tracking inside Essentials.
  • Plain-English monthly guidance.
  • A single account across the workbook and app.
