## How to Test (So You Don't Burn Budget)

1.  **Pick 3 hooks from Tier 1** (1 contrarian, 1 math/anchor, 1 confessional). That's your first ad set.
2.  **One visual per hook** — don't test 5 visuals at once. FinTok data shows hook > production.
3.  **$20/day on Meta Advantage+ or TikTok** — 3 ad groups, $6-7 each, broad targeting (22-45, US, interests: YNAB, budgeting, personal finance, Excel).
4.  **Kill rule:** Under 0.85% CTR (Meta finance benchmark) or under 0.4% (TikTok) after $15 spend → kill. Over → scale.
5.  **Winner gets 70% of budget** on day 5. Use remaining 20% to test next Tier 1 hook.

**What to measure beyond CTR:** Finance TikTok CVR is ~0.4-1% and CPA is $50-170 for apps. Your $19 digital product should aim for $8-12 CPA on cold traffic if creative is strong — if CPA is $18+, hook or landing page is the problem, not budget.
