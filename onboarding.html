FOLLOW YOUR WORKFLOW

Choose your edition

Every edition starts with Budget Input. The difference is where debt, planning, and long-term tracking happen next.

MONTHLY CLARITY

Essentials

  1. Replace the sample rows on Budget Input.
  2. Enter debt payments once under the Debt Payments category.
  3. Review totals, Feedback, subscriptions, and charts.

DEBT & ASSET ENGINE

Complete

  1. Complete Budget Input, then enter each debt on Debt Payoff.
  2. Use Interest Saved to test extra-payment scenarios.
  3. Build Net Worth and test Mortgage Estimator scenarios.

THE FINANCIAL SYSTEM

Premium

  1. Complete Budget Input, Debt Payoff, Net Worth, and Emergency Fund.
  2. Use Unified Dashboard and the Financial Health Score as your compass.
  3. Track Annual Review and project your investment goal.

SAFE SETUP

Replace examples before trusting results.

Yellow and gold cells are intended for your inputs. Light-blue cells contain assumptions or benchmarks you can adjust. Avoid typing over totals, feedback, or chart-source areas.

Final first-session check

  • ✓ Income, expenses, and surplus match your expectations.
  • ✓ Weekly and annual figures were converted to monthly amounts.
  • ✓ Debt payments were entered only once.
  • ✓ You saved your working copy after reviewing the results.
